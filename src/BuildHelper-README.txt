BuildHelper for Brick by Brick
https://github.com/michel86ch-ui/brickbybrick-mod-buildhelper

INSTALL
Unzip everything into the game folder, so that winhttp.dll sits next to BrickByBrick.exe
(Steam: right-click the game > Manage > Browse local files). BepInEx already installed
(e.g. with Extra Buildings or the Ladder)? Just overwrite, the files are the same.

HOW TO USE
F8: show / hide the empty brick places of sites with up to 50 bricks missing (magenta, through walls).
F5: ghost hand. Carry the brick and look towards its place, walls in between do not matter.
F5 marks the place in cyan, F5 again within 5 seconds places the brick. Once every 10 minutes;
the countdown is an icon in the skill bar, left of sprint.
Settings: BepInEx\config\michel.brickbybrick.buildhelper.cfg (created at the first game start).
Note: a world in which the ghost hand placed a brick no longer counts for the Steam leaderboard.

UNINSTALL
Delete the folder BepInEx\plugins\BuildHelper.

Includes BepInEx 5.4.23.5, unmodified (MIT license: BepInEx\LICENSE-BepInEx.txt).
Unofficial fan mod, not made by the game's developers.
